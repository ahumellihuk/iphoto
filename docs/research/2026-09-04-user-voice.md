## Recurring themes

- Videos are the culprit and the ceiling is real: "videos are the main culprit", "4K 60FPS", "99% full", "no upgrade plans above 2TB", "2TB of media a year".
- Device is smaller than the library: "laptop doesn't have enough space to download everything", "512GB Mac / 1TB library". Optimize Storage is both the fix and a trap.
- Fear of loss dominates: "Please don't tell me this can't be done", "risk losing them if the hard drive breaks", "repopulate", "40k photos now gone", "NO FILES", "Once lost, no amount of money can recover it", "losing 10 years of photos".
- Optimize Mac Storage is misunderstood: "What do I back up then?", "won't recognize the thumbnails".
- Workarounds are painful: 1,000-photo limit on iCloud.com, no Select All on iPad, .MOV export failures, lost dates, "connect my external drive every time", Apple data export "takes a couple days".
- Nag fatigue: "Obnoxious prompt", "constant iCloud Storage Full message".
- Vocabulary: full / 99% full / completely full, main culprit, offload, archive / archiving, local copy, start over, TRANSFER not copy, originals vs thumbnails / low res, repopulate, out of step, inconvenient, absurd, clumsy, obnoxious, upset and confused, gone / lost / missing.

## Explicit feature requests seen

- Incremental sync of the Photos library to an external drive, preserving albums and folders (MacRumors 2026).
- Keep Optimize Storage on but download the whole library "straight to an external drive" (MacRumors 2024).
- Per-album Download Originals while the rest stays optimized (MacRumors 2022).
- An "online only folder" in iCloud that does not sync to devices (Apple Community 2021).
- A NAS that connects to iCloud accounts to retain copies (MacRumors 2022).
- Bigger plans: 4, 6, 8, 10, 20 TB.
- Tools people reach for: icloudpd, osxphotos, Photos Takeout, Happy Photo Exporter, FileBrowser Pro, Google and Amazon Photos, QNAP NAS, rsync, Time Machine, Apple's data export.
