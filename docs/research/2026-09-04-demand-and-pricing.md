# Research: demand, market size, and pricing (2026-09-04)

Agent-gathered. Numbers marked [est.] are estimates; everything else is sourced. Reddit blocked the crawler and YouTube returned no view counts.

## 1. Demand signals

- Keyword volume: "iCloud" is the most-searched cloud storage brand at 87.9k US searches/month (Semrush 2025) ([connectbit.com](https://connectbit.com/cloud-storage-statistics/)). Long-tail phrases are commercially contested: MacPaw, Cloudwards, TechRadar, Tidy all maintain dedicated "iCloud storage full" pages ([macpaw.com](https://macpaw.com/how-to/free-up-icloud-storage-full), [cloudwards.net](https://www.cloudwards.net/solved-icloud-storage-full/), [techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/how-to/is-your-icloud-storage-full-5-tips-on-how-to-clear-it-to-free-up-space), [tidy.gallery](https://tidy.gallery/blog/free-up-icloud-storage/)).
- Survey evidence (strongest quantified signal): Western Digital World Backup Day survey (n=6,118, 10 countries, Feb 2025): 60% ran out of cloud space in the past six months, 78% rely on free tiers, 67% back up specifically to free device space, 56% upgraded to a paid plan, 35% find cloud "increasingly expensive", 45% use an external hard drive ([westerndigital.com](https://www.westerndigital.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2025/2025-03-25-western-digital-world-backup-day)). SanDisk survey: 65% have regretted deleting a photo or video to free space ([thehansindia.com](https://www.thehansindia.com/posts/index/education-and-careers/2018-04-11/indian-users-regularly-run-out-of-space/373235)). TechRadar: "millions of Americans" delete photos yearly for lack of storage ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/pro/billions-of-cherished-photos-at-risk-only-a-third-of-americans-back-up-their-precious-pics-to-the-cloud)).
- YouTube: 10 distinct tutorials on moving iPhone photos/videos to an external drive, five published Dec 2025 to May 2026 ([example 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=scU5d76B8IQ), [example 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ytSnP4XDeXM), [example 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oLOdTsEWyCg)). View counts not retrievable.
- Forums: MacRumors Nov 2025 thread "Best way to backup 80GB of photos from phone to external drive?" got only native-tool answers and no app recommendation ([forums.macrumors.com](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/best-way-to-backup-80gb-of-photos-from-phone-16pm-to-external-drive.2471665/)).
- Apple Support maintains at least four articles on this pain: [108922](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922), [105061](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105061), [108345](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108345), [108306](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306). Prescribed remedies are only "delete content" or "upgrade to iCloud+".

## 2. Market size

- 1.561B active iPhones worldwide, 150.7M in the US ([backlinko.com](https://backlinko.com/iphone-users)).
- CIRP: about 64-67% of US Apple customers pay for iCloud storage, versus 42% Apple Music ([macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/08/21/icloud-storage-most-popular-apple-service/), [appleinsider.com](https://appleinsider.com/articles/24/08/21/icloud-storage-leads-the-pack-in-apples-services-ecosystem)).
- Library sizes: no authoritative survey. Self-reported MacRumors data ranges 4GB to 500GB, median about 30-35GB ([forums.macrumors.com](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/what-is-the-size-of-your-photos-library-on-your-ios-devices.2078974/)). Per-file: HEIC about 3MB; 4K30 video about 170MB/min, 4K60 about 400MB/min ([tidy.gallery](https://tidy.gallery/blog/iphone-storage-full-photos/), [cleanor.app](https://cleanor.app/blog/how-many-photos-fit-in-your-phone-storage-capacity)). Video share of bytes: 60-80% [est.].

What the market tolerates: consumer storage-relief utilities cluster at $10-45 one-time. Subscriptions above about $40/yr are tolerated only for apps that replace a cloud or are creative tools. Indie guidance for 2026: annual $20-40 with lifetime at 3-4x ([appopportunity.com](https://appopportunity.com/blog/indie-app-revenue-models-2026)).

"Pays for itself": a family at the 2TB ceiling faces +$240/yr to reach 6TB. A $39 app plus an $85 2TB drive (about $125) pays back in about 6 months against that jump, and in about 18 months against the 200GB to 2TB step (+$84/yr). Recommended [est.]: $29-39 one-time with a $49-59 Family tier, optional ~$15/yr updates.

## 4. External drive market

Typical family purchase is a 2-5TB portable HDD or 1-2TB SSD: Seagate Portable 2TB with 272k reviews, from $84; WD My Passport 4TB about $120; Samsung T7 as premium pick; 2TB holds about 500 hours of HD video ([thirstybear.com](https://www.thirstybear.com/best-external-hard-drives/), [tomshardware.com](https://www.tomshardware.com/reviews/best-external-hard-drive-ssd,5987.html), [pcworld.com](https://www.pcworld.com/article/406861/best-external-drives.html)). Apple sells LaCie and SanDisk drives on apple.com positioned as pro accessories ([apple.com LaCie](https://www.apple.com/shop/mac/accessories/drives-storage/lacie)).

## 5. What success looks like for a niche Mac utility

- Lunar (monitor brightness): $7,000/mo as of May 2026 from a $23 lifetime license; paid ads produced zero sales; Product Hunt, HN, r/macapps drove everything ([indiehustle.co](https://www.indiehustle.co/p/no-ads-no-funnels-no-black-friday)). Studio 2023 books: $108.4k gross, about $70k net; intermediaries take about 36% ([notes.alinpanaitiu.com](https://notes.alinpanaitiu.com/Costs-of-running-a-macOS-app-studio-business)).
- Xnapper (screenshots): $29 one-time, $3-6k/mo, sold for $150k ([indiehackers.com](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tony-dinh-on-how-he-runs-3-profitable-projects-simultaneously-879035fb2e), [boringcashcow.com](https://boringcashcow.com/check/xnapper.com)).
- Medians: solo indie apps typically under $1k/mo; top quartile $3-15k/mo after 12-18 months; 81% never cross $1k/mo ([forasoft.com](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/app-revenue-potential), [anything.com](https://www.anything.com/blog/indie-app-ideas-with-real-earning-potential)).

Calibration [est.]: a focused $30-40 utility solving a recurring, emotionally charged pain with clear pays-for-itself math has a realistic ceiling of $3-8k/month (100-200 sales/month).

Key caveats: Mac ownership among iPhone owners (35%) and video byte share (60-80%) are unsourced; Reddit and YouTube counts not fetched; the 450M global paid-iCloud figure is low quality.
